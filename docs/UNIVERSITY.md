# University and GPA

## Courses

Optional metadata: university, professor, academic year, semester, CFU. Nothing is required except a name.

## Topics

Ordered topics with status: not started, in progress, completed. Study time can be associated with a topic.

## Documents

Files stay on your computer. BluePhoenix stores names and local paths for this device. Nothing is uploaded to Neon.

## Attendance vs study

Lessons record date, duration, attended/missed. Study time is the timer. They are independent metrics.

## Exams

Attempts can be scheduled, attempted, passed, failed, withdrawn, or no-show. A grade is optional.

The **final grade is stored explicitly**. BluePhoenix will not pick your highest attempt for you.

## GPA

Italian 30-point scale by default, passing 18.

Weighted average = Σ(grade × CFU) / Σ(CFU) for courses that have a final grade. Failed courses are excluded unless you enable that in settings.

If some included courses have no CFU, the UI falls back to a simple average and tells you which courses were included or skipped.
